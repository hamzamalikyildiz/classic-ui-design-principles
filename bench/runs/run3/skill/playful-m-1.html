Three ways to play

Crayon Studio

Thick, chunky brushes that never run out. Kids draw on a big canvas, pick a colour from the crayon tray, and undo with one big round button.

Tap to animate

One tap makes a drawing wiggle, swim or fly. Shapes learn how they move, so a fish swims and a bird flaps.

Share with family

Send a link to grandparents or pin a favourite to the family wall. Nothing is public unless a grown-up says so.

Stories that grow

Drop three drawings in a row and Doodlebug builds a short cartoon, with the sound of your own voice as the narrator.
