Three reasons kids stay

Animate in one tap

Every drawing gets a bouncy walk cycle. Kids pick from 12 motions: hop, spin, swim, fly. Nothing to rig, nothing to learn.

A safe, ad-free space

No ads, no in-app purchases, no chat with strangers. Art stays private until a parent says yes.

Brushes that grow

Draw every day to unlock 40 brushes, from glitter crayon to watercolor. Each one feels different under a small finger.
