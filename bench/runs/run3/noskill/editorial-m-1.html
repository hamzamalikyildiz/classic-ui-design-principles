Weeknight, Unhurried

Brown Butter Lemon Pasta, Made Slowly

Ten minutes of work, twenty of watching. The sauce comes from patience, not from a long ingredient list.

Serves four · 25 minutes

Why brown butter

My grandmother never measured anything, but she always waited for the butter to turn. She would swirl the pan, watch the foam settle, and wait for the smell that tells you the milk solids have gone toasted and nutty. That smell is the whole recipe.

Lemon belongs here because it cuts through the richness. A good zest, a little juice, and a spoon of pasta water turn the butter into a sauce that clings instead of pooling. Nothing is complicated. The skill is restraint: take the pan off the heat a moment before you think you should.

“The pan will tell you when it is ready, if you stop hurrying long enough to listen.”

From the kitchen notebook

Ingredients

For four generous bowls

  • Spaghetti or bucatini400 g
  • Unsalted butter120 g
  • Garlic, thinly sliced3 cloves
  • Lemons, zested and juiced2
  • Pecorino Romano, finely grated80 g
  • Black pepper, cracked1 tsp
  • Fine sea saltto taste

Method

  1. 1

    Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and salt it generously. Cook the pasta two minutes short of the packet time. Reserve a full cup of the cooking water before draining.

  2. 2

    While the pasta cooks, melt the butter in a wide, light-coloured pan over medium heat. Let it foam, then swirl it steadily until the foam drops and the solids turn amber.

  3. 3

    Add the garlic and cook for about a minute, until fragrant but not coloured. Pull the pan off the heat before the butter darkens past hazelnut.

  4. 4

    Add the drained pasta, a splash of reserved water, the lemon zest and juice, and most of the cheese. Toss vigorously over low heat until the sauce turns glossy and clings to every strand.

  5. 5

    Taste, adjust with salt and more water if it tightens, then divide among warm bowls. Finish with pepper and the last of the cheese.

Cook's note: Brown butter goes from perfect to bitter in seconds. Keep the pan moving and have the lemon ready before the butter starts to foam.
