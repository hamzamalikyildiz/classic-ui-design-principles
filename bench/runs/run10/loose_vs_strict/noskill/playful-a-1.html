For parents

Questions we hear most

Is it safe for my child?+

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases aimed at kids, no chat with strangers and no public profiles. We store only the progress needed for lessons.

How much time a day does it take?+

About five minutes. Lessons are short on purpose, so kids finish before they lose interest.

Do they need to read well first?+

No. Every word is paired with a picture and read aloud, so early readers can follow along with a grown-up or on their own.

Can I see what they learned?+

Yes. A weekly email lists the words learned, the streak and the lessons to try next. You can also turn the email off.

Ready for the first hop?

Give your child one free lesson today. It takes five minutes and no card.
