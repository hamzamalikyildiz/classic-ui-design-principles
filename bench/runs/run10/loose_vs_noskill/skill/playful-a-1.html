Questions from grown-ups

Is Hoppity safe for my child?+

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases aimed at kids, and no chat with other players. We never sell children's data.

How much time does it take each day?+

About 5 to 10 minutes. Lessons are short on purpose, so a bedtime hop works as well as a morning one.

Do I need to speak the language?+

Not at all. Every lesson shows a picture and reads the words aloud, so you can learn alongside your child.

Can I cancel any time?+

Yes. Cancel in two taps from settings. Your child keeps their progress on the free plan.
