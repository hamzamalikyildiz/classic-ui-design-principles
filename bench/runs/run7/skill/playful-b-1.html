Sat 14 and Sun 15 March · Harbour Hall

Go wild for science!

Two days of splashing, building, launching and poking around with 40 hands-on activities for kids aged 3 to 12.

Get your tickets See the zones

Four zones, one giant playground

Every activity is run by a real scientist or a trained helper. Wander freely, nothing needs booking.

Build Lab

Stack, balance and wire up cardboard robots. Kid-sized tools, and the bridge test ends in a splash.

Ages 3 and up · Open all day

Splash Lab

Dam rivers, sink boats and time how fast water rushes down a pipe.

Ages 4 and up

Sky Lab

Build foam rockets and launch them from the pad. Winners get a glow-in-the-dark badge.

Ages 6 and up

Bug Garden

Meet stick insects, giant snails and a honeybee hive behind glass. Keepers answer every question.

Ages 3 and up · Talks at 13:00 and 15:30

Safety first, fun second

Our staff check every zone every 30 minutes. Here is what we do to keep little explorers safe.

  • Wristbands for under-5sParent contact details on every band.
  • Lifeguard at the waterNon-slip mats and a dry towel station.
  • Allergy-labelled foodNut-free tent with ingredients on display.
  • First aid by Sky LabStaffed tent open for the whole weekend.
