Safety first

Simple rules keep the fun going for everyone.

  • Stay with your childChildren under 8 must be supervised at all times.
  • Goggles onSafety goggles are required near the volcano and bubble tunnel.
  • Allergy awareOur labs are nut free. Tell staff about any allergies at the door.
  • Lost? Find a helperStaff in yellow vests can reunite you in minutes.
