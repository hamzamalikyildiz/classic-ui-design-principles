Tomatoes, Slowly Coaxed Into Something Sweeter

Two hours in a low oven turn a grocery-store pint into jam-like pools, and a can of beans becomes supper.

By Nadia Okafor · Serves 4 · 1 hour 45 minutes, mostly unattended

Every summer a pint of cherry tomatoes arrives at my kitchen and, within a few days, starts to sulk. The skins wrinkle, the flavour goes flat, and the optimistic bowl on the counter begins to look like a small reproach. For years I threw them into salads and hoped for the best.

The answer turned out to be patience. Set the oven low, tip the tomatoes into a shallow dish with a generous glug of oil, and leave them alone. Over two hours the water leaves, the sugars concentrate, and the edges catch in a way that tastes like the fruit has remembered what it was for. The kitchen smells faintly of jam and something sharper underneath.

White beans are the obvious partner, since they soak up the pan juices without any fuss. A tin of cannellini, warmed in the same oil, becomes a soft base for the tomatoes. Lemon zest does the rest, cutting through the sweetness and making the whole plate feel lighter than it has any right to.

This is a dish for a Tuesday, not a showcase. Put the oven on, answer some email, and come back to something that tastes as though you planned it for a week.

“The tomato is not in a hurry, and neither should the cook be.”

From the recipe notes, pinned to the fridge

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oven to 150°C (130°C fan). Halve 500 g cherry tomatoes and spread them cut side up in a shallow dish with 4 crushed garlic cloves and a pinch of salt.

  2. 2

    Pour over 80 ml good olive oil, a few thyme sprigs and a teaspoon of sugar. Roast for 1 hour 45 minutes, until collapsed and the edges are dark gold.

  3. 3

    Meanwhile, warm 2 tins of drained cannellini beans in a small pan with 3 tablespoons of the roasting oil, a splash of water and a little black pepper, for about 6 minutes.

  4. 4

    Spoon the beans onto a warm plate, lay the tomatoes and their juices over the top, then finish with the zest of one lemon, a squeeze of its juice and torn basil.

Ingredients

For four, as a light main or generous side

  • Cherry tomatoes500 g
  • Olive oil, good80 ml
  • Garlic4 cloves
  • Thyme4 sprigs
  • Caster sugar1 tsp
  • Cannellini beans, tinned2 tins
  • Lemon1
  • Basil1 small bunch
  • Sea salt and black pepperto taste
