# Trust mood notes

- One accent plus tinted neutrals, `shadow-sm`, 12-16px radius, one hero accent card/band per screen.
- Trust does not mean generic. Take the accent from the subject (forest green and brass for money, deep teal for health, ink navy for legal), not stock blue; tint the neutrals toward it; give headings a serif or distinctive display face and keep a clean sans for data (`reference/visual-quality.md`).
- Give the page a data story, not just KPI cards: an annotated chart (event marker or threshold line with a one-line explanation), a status band for the one thing that needs attention (overdue invoice, failed payment), and a ranked list with trend sparklines.
- Fill every panel (see Technical notes on half-empty panels); tables show 8+ rows with `tabular-nums`, charts have labelled axes and a legend.
- Semantic colors carry state (red/amber/green) and always come with text or an icon.
- One signature detail so it is not a template: a ledger-paper tint, a real-looking statement preview, date chips, or a hand-drawn empty state.

Content floor: top bar or page header with actions, 4+ KPIs with deltas, a multi-series annotated chart with legend, a ranked or status list, a transactions/schedule table (8+ rows, tabular-nums), one action-needed/overdue alert, an empty/loading state, and a statement or detail panel where relevant.
